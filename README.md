# Americantriangle

**Building Intelligence. Engineering Possibility.**

Americantriangle is an AI technology company focused on connecting data infrastructure, AI research, and enterprise engineering to advance practical artificial intelligence.

We bring together the capabilities needed to develop, improve, evaluate, and deploy AI systems — combining technical expertise, human feedback, and innovative engineering.

## Our Three Core Divisions

Americantriangle is built around three complementary divisions that work together as one connected AI ecosystem.

### 1. AI Data Infrastructure
*The Foundation*

Providing the high-quality data and human expertise that AI systems need to learn and improve.

- Data collection and annotation
- Human feedback and RLHF
- Training data preparation
- Data curation and quality management

### 2. AI Model Training, Research & Evaluation
*The Intelligence*

Developing, improving, and evaluating AI models to make them more capable, reliable, and effective.

- Model training and fine-tuning
- AI research and experimentation
- Model benchmarking
- Testing and evaluation
- AI safety, alignment, and red-teaming

### 3. Enterprise AI Applications
*The Implementation*

Turning AI capabilities into practical solutions that address real business needs.

- AI agents and automation
- Custom AI applications
- Enterprise integrations
- AI deployment and operations

## One Connected Ecosystem

Our three divisions are independently valuable and mutually reinforcing.

Data infrastructure supports AI development. Research and evaluation help improve model capabilities. Enterprise applications bring AI into real-world environments, generating new requirements and feedback that can inform further improvements.

Together, these capabilities connect the foundations of AI development with practical business applications.

## Our Mission

To help organizations develop, improve, evaluate, and deploy reliable AI systems through integrated technology capabilities and human expertise.

## Our Vision

To build a connected AI ecosystem that bridges data, intelligence, and engineering — making advanced AI more useful and accessible to organizations.

## What We're Building

Americantriangle is developing its technology platform, business capabilities, and collaborative ecosystem to support AI innovation and enterprise adoption.

Our long-term direction is to become a trusted technology partner for organizations working across the AI lifecycle.
